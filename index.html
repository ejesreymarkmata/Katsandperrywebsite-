A neighborhood cafe that stays open late

Kat & Perry’s serves house-baked donuts, well-made coffee and comforting pasta in a bright, easygoing space. Sit inside where it’s cool, or take a table in the garden under the trees.

The kitchen takes last orders at 11pm, so it works for an afternoon coffee and a late dinner alike.
